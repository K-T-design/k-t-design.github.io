🤔 What is ssi263-speech?

The SSI-263 was a legendary speech synthesis chip used in devices from the 1980s and 1990s. Devices like the Speak-Out, Braille Lite, Accent-mini, and Accent SA all relied on this chip to convert text into spoken words. Now, you can experience those classic voices on your modern Windows computer.

This application is a faithful emulator of that original chip. It recreates the exact sound and behavior of the hardware, running the original firmware from those devices. It's like having a piece of computing history running right on your PC.

Whether you're a retro computing enthusiast, a developer interested in speech synthesis, or someone who simply misses those unique robot-like voices, this tool brings the past to life.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the real SSI-263 chip?

No, it's a software emulation of the chip. It recreates the exact behavior and sound of the hardware using software. This means no vintage hardware is needed!

Can I use this with screen readers?

Yes! The included NVDA add-ons allow you to use the SSI-263 voices as a screen reader voice in NVDA, giving you a completely refreshing experience.

Why does the voice sound "robotic"?

That's the charm of retro speech synthesis! The SSI-263 produced speech by combining phonemes and formants digitally. It's not trying to sound human; it's designed to be clear and iconic in its own unique way.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

This version is specifically designed for Windows. Keep an eye on the project repository for future updates and potential ports.

Is it really free?

Absolutely yes. This is an open-source project. You can use it for personal or even commercial purposes, all at no cost. Support the developer by starring the repository on GitHub.
